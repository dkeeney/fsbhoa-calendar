Finally, an HOA Calendar That Just Works. Built by an HOA volunteer for effortless community scheduling, mobile syncing, and custom Canva themes.

== Description ==

Managing a thriving community schedule shouldn't require advanced computer skills or hours of tedious layout work. A great neighborhood calendar should make it effortless to move an unexpected board meeting, coordinate multi-day pool events, and ensure that every single resident can read the timeline perfectly—whether they are sitting at a desktop computer or scrolling on their smartphone.

The **HOAplugin Calendar** was built from scratch by an HOA volunteer who understands how neighborhood schedules actually operate. It gives you a beautiful, professional website calendar without needing a lot of computer skills.

### Why You’ll Love It (Free Version Features)

* **True "Point-and-Click" Editing:** You don’t need to know how to code. If you can type a title and pick a color, managing the calendar is as simple as filling out a basic form.
* **Keep the Whole Year Organized:** A live, interactive calendar allows your Social Committee to lock in dates early in the year. Residents can easily click back and forth through the months to see what’s coming up.
* **"Add to My Calendar" Phone Sync:** With a single click, residents can instantly sync the date, time, and location directly into their personal phone calendar (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook). No more "I forgot that was tonight" excuses!
* **Built-In Magnifier for Easy Reading:** If a specific day gets crowded with multiple meetings, simply hover your mouse over the day square and it gently expands so you can read every detail effortlessly.
* **Protect Resident Privacy:** Easily check a box to mark private committee sessions, financial reviews, or neighborhood meetings as "Resident-Only." These events stay hidden from the public internet.
* **Handles Annoying HOA Schedules with Ease:** Easily schedule complex repeating events (e.g., the last Wednesday of the month, the 2nd and 4th Saturdays, or every MWF for pool aerobics).
* **Handy Community Reminders:** Set up visual icons that display right in the corner of the calendar day squares (e.g., a brown trash can every Monday, a blue can for recycling, or a street sweeper icon).
* **Never Squint on a Phone Again:** Automatically detects screen size. On a phone, it instantly flips into a neat, vertical agenda list that residents can scroll through effortlessly.
* **Add Your Community Flyers:** Link a PDF or image flyer directly to the event so it pops right up when clicked.
* **Custom Themes with Canva:** Completely customize the look of each month's page using beautiful graphics designed in Canva. Import your background, and the calendar automatically overlays your events perfectly.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `hoaplugin-calendar` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install directly through the WordPress plugins screen.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.
3. Navigate to the new HOAplugin Calendar menu in your dashboard to begin adding events.
4. Place the `[hoaplugin_calendar]` shortcode on any page or post to display the calendar to your residents.
